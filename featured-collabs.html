Cover Story

Project Neon

October 2024 • Fashion Editorial

In a world where physical boundaries are dissolving, Project Neon explores the intersection of street style and virtual reality textures. We partnered with CyberVogue to create a collection that exists solely in the metaverse.

Using generative AI, we designed fabrics that defy the laws of physics—shimmering liquid metals, bioluminescent weaves, and gravity-defying silhouettes. The result is a campaign that challenges our perception of what fashion can be.

"We didn't just want to create clothes. We wanted to create a feeling. A digital emotion."
