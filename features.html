Fina x Jessie – Dreamwave Series

We dove into early 2000s Y2K fashion, styled with soft lens flares and pastel overlays. Jessie brought incredible energy and her own digital wardrobe.

We captured this look on a mirrored rooftop set built in MidJourney and fine-tuned in Photoshop. The shadows hit just right.

This portrait shows the fine blend between virtual fabric and skin texture overlays. We wanted surreal realism here.

Fina x Clara – Gothic Glitch

Dark mode meets cyberpunk Tokyo. Clara and I created a broken mirror narrative using glitch textures, velvet lace, and electric shadows.

This shoot was done in multiple aspect ratios, styled to feel disjointed but elegant.
